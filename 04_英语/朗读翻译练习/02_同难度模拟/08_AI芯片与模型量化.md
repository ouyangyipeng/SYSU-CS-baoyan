# AI芯片与模型量化

类型：同难度模拟。根据常见计算机英语题材自编的模拟练习，没有原题命中的含义。

涉及公司政策、产业趋势的内容按原练习的历史背景阅读，不作为当前新闻。

## English

> The rapid growth of neural networks has increased the demand for specialized AI chips. Unlike general-purpose processors, these chips are designed to perform large numbers of matrix operations in parallel. Their speed, however, depends not only on arithmetic units but also on how efficiently data moves through memory. Model quantization reduces the number of bits used to represent weights or activations, which can lower memory use and increase throughput on supported hardware. The trade-off is that rounding errors may reduce model accuracy, especially for sensitive layers. Engineers must therefore choose suitable numerical formats, calibrate the model, and evaluate both speed and quality. Lower precision is useful only when the hardware and software can execute it efficiently.

## 参考翻译

神经网络规模快速增长，推动了专用 AI 芯片的需求。与通用处理器不同，这些芯片面向大量并行矩阵运算设计。不过，芯片速度不仅取决于计算单元，也取决于数据在内存层次中的移动效率。模型量化减少权重或激活值的表示位数，在硬件支持时可以降低内存占用并提高吞吐量。代价是舍入误差可能降低模型精度，尤其会影响较敏感的网络层。因此，工程师需要选择合适的数值格式、校准模型，并同时评估速度与质量。只有软硬件能够高效执行低精度运算时，降低精度才真正有用。
