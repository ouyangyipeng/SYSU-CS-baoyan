## English

> An autonomous vehicle must understand its surroundings before it can plan a safe route. Cameras provide rich information about lanes, traffic lights, and pedestrians, but their performance may decline in darkness or heavy rain. Radar measures distance and speed reliably, while LiDAR produces accurate three-dimensional maps at a higher cost. Modern perception systems therefore combine data from several sensors, a process known as sensor fusion. The sensors, however, do not always agree, and even a small timing error can place an object in the wrong position. Researchers are developing calibration methods and uncertainty-aware models to make fusion more reliable. A remaining challenge is to maintain both accuracy and real-time performance when the vehicle enters an unfamiliar environment.

## 参考翻译

自动驾驶汽车必须先理解周围环境，才能规划安全路线。摄像头能够提供车道、交通灯和行人等丰富信息，但在黑暗或大雨中性能可能下降。雷达能够可靠地测量距离和速度，而激光雷达可以生成精确的三维地图，但成本更高。因此，现代感知系统通常融合多种传感器的数据，这一过程称为传感器融合。不过，不同传感器并不总能得出一致结果，即使很小的时间误差也可能导致物体位置判断错误。研究人员正在开发校准方法和能够表示不确定性的模型，以提高融合可靠性。如何在陌生环境中同时保持精度和实时性，仍是重要挑战。
