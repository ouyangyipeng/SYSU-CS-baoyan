# 分布式训练

类型：同难度模拟。根据常见计算机英语题材自编的模拟练习，没有原题命中的含义。

涉及公司政策、产业趋势的内容按原练习的历史背景阅读，不作为当前新闻。

## English

> Training a large model may require hundreds of workers to compute gradients on different parts of the data. At the end of each step, the system usually waits for their results before updating the model. A worker that becomes slow because of network congestion or resource competition is called a straggler. Even one straggler can delay the entire step, leaving expensive accelerators idle. Possible solutions include assigning backup tasks, ignoring a limited number of late results, or predicting which workers are likely to slow down. These methods reduce waiting time but may increase computation or affect convergence. The best strategy depends on whether delays are occasional, persistent, or related to particular data and machines.

## 参考翻译

训练大型模型可能需要数百个工作节点分别在不同数据上计算梯度。每一步结束时，系统通常要等待各节点返回结果后才能更新模型。由于网络拥塞或资源竞争而变慢的节点称为掉队节点。即使只有一个掉队节点，也可能拖慢整个训练步骤，使昂贵的加速器处于空闲状态。解决方案包括分配备份任务、忽略少量迟到结果，或者预测哪些节点容易变慢。这些方法能减少等待时间，但也可能增加计算量或影响收敛。最佳策略取决于延迟是偶发、长期存在，还是与特定数据和机器有关。

## 抓词与长句

- gradient 梯度；straggler 掉队节点；congestion 拥塞；idle 空闲的；convergence 收敛。
- 注意 `which workers are likely to slow down` 是 predict 的宾语从句。
