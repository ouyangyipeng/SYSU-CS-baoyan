# 语义分割

类型：同难度模拟。根据常见计算机英语题材自编的模拟练习，没有原题命中的含义。

涉及公司政策、产业趋势的内容按原练习的历史背景阅读，不作为当前新闻。

## English

> Image classification assigns one label to an entire picture, but many applications need to know the category of every pixel. Semantic segmentation provides this detailed understanding by dividing an image into regions such as roads, buildings, people, and vehicles. Deep networks have greatly improved segmentation accuracy, especially when they combine local image details with broader contextual information. Nevertheless, objects with similar colors may be confused, and small or partly hidden objects remain difficult to identify. Real-time applications must also process video frames with limited delay. Collecting pixel-level labels is expensive because annotators must trace object boundaries carefully. Researchers are exploring weak supervision and synthetic data to reduce this cost while preserving performance in real scenes.

## 参考翻译

图像分类为整张图片分配一个标签，但许多应用还需要知道每个像素所属的类别。语义分割通过把图像划分为道路、建筑、行人和车辆等区域，提供更细致的理解。深度网络显著提高了分割精度，尤其是同时结合局部图像细节和更广泛上下文时。不过，颜色相近的物体可能被混淆，小物体或部分遮挡的物体也仍然难以识别。实时应用还必须在较低延迟下处理视频帧。逐像素标注的采集成本很高，因为标注者必须仔细勾画物体边界。研究人员正尝试弱监督和合成数据，以在保持真实场景性能的同时降低标注成本。
