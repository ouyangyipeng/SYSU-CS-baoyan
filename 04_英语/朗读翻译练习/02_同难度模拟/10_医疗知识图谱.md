# 医疗知识图谱

类型：同难度模拟。根据常见计算机英语题材自编的模拟练习，没有原题命中的含义。

涉及公司政策、产业趋势的内容按原练习的历史背景阅读，不作为当前新闻。

## English

> Medical information is scattered across research papers, clinical records, and specialized databases, making it difficult to connect related evidence. A knowledge graph represents entities such as diseases, drugs, and symptoms as nodes, while their relationships form edges. This structure can support search, question answering, and clinical decision tools by making connections explicit. In many systems, each relationship is linked to its supporting documents. Constructing a reliable graph is challenging because medical terms may have several names, and extracted relationships can be uncertain or outdated. Errors are particularly serious when the graph is used to support treatment decisions. For this reason, medical knowledge graphs require expert review, clear evidence sources, and regular updates rather than relying entirely on automatic extraction.

## 参考翻译

医学信息分散在论文、临床记录和专业数据库中，很难把相关证据连接起来。知识图谱把疾病、药物和症状等实体表示为节点，把它们之间的关系表示为边。由于关系被显式表达，这种结构能够支持搜索、问答和临床决策工具。在许多系统中，每条关系还会链接到支持它的文档。可靠图谱的构建并不容易，因为同一个医学术语可能有多个名称，自动抽取的关系也可能不确定或已经过时。当图谱用于辅助治疗决策时，错误尤其严重。因此，医学知识图谱需要专家审核、清晰的证据来源和定期更新，而不能完全依赖自动抽取。
