# 节能数据中心

类型：同难度模拟。根据常见计算机英语题材自编的模拟练习，没有原题命中的含义。

涉及公司政策、产业趋势的内容按原练习的历史背景阅读，不作为当前新闻。

## English

> Data centers consume large amounts of electricity not only to run servers but also to cool them. Operators can reduce energy use by moving workloads to machines with higher utilization and turning idle servers off. This approach sounds simple, yet frequent migration may interrupt applications and create extra network traffic. Renewable energy adds another opportunity because flexible tasks can be scheduled when solar or wind power is available. However, delaying too much work may violate service requirements. Energy-aware scheduling must therefore balance power consumption, performance, and reliability. The goal is not merely to use fewer machines, but to make careful decisions based on workload demand, electricity supply, and the cost of moving computation.

## 参考翻译

数据中心消耗大量电力，不仅用于运行服务器，也用于冷却设备。运营方可以把任务迁移到利用率更高的机器，并关闭空闲服务器以减少能耗。这个方法看似简单，但频繁迁移可能中断应用并产生额外网络流量。可再生能源提供了另一种机会，因为可灵活调度的任务可以安排在太阳能或风能充足时执行。然而，过度延迟任务可能违反服务要求。因此，节能调度必须在功耗、性能和可靠性之间取得平衡。目标不只是减少机器数量，而是根据负载需求、电力供应和计算迁移成本作出谨慎决策。

## 抓词与长句

- utilization 利用率；migration 迁移；renewable energy 可再生能源；violate 违反；workload 负载。
- `not merely..., but...`：不只是……，而是……。
