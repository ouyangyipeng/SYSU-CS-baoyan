# 后量子密码学

类型：扩展题材。根据常见计算机英语题材自编的模拟练习，没有原题命中的含义。

涉及公司政策、产业趋势的内容按原练习的历史背景阅读，不作为当前新闻。

## English

> Many public-key cryptographic systems depend on mathematical problems that are extremely difficult for ordinary computers to solve. A sufficiently powerful quantum computer could perform some of these calculations much faster, threatening encryption and digital signatures used today. Post-quantum cryptography develops new algorithms based on problems that are believed to be difficult for both classical and quantum computers. These algorithms can run on existing computers, so they do not require users to own quantum hardware. Replacing cryptographic infrastructure will still take years because protocols, software, devices, and stored data must all be considered. Organizations are therefore beginning the transition before large quantum computers capable of breaking current systems actually exist.

## 参考翻译

许多公钥密码系统依赖普通计算机极难解决的数学问题。足够强大的量子计算机可能更快完成其中部分计算，从而威胁今天使用的加密和数字签名。后量子密码学研究新的算法，这些算法所基于的问题被认为对传统计算机和量子计算机都很困难。它们可以在现有计算机上运行，因此用户不需要拥有量子硬件。不过，替换密码基础设施仍需要多年，因为协议、软件、设备和已存储数据都必须纳入考虑。因此，各机构正在真正能够破解现有系统的大型量子计算机出现之前，提前开始迁移。
