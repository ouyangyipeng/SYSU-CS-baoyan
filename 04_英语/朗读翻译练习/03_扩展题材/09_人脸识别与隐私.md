## English

> Facial recognition systems identify or verify a person by comparing features extracted from an image with stored face data. They can unlock phones, help organize photographs, or assist security checks, but their use in public spaces has caused concern. Accuracy may vary across lighting conditions, camera angles, ages, and demographic groups, creating a risk of unequal error rates. A false match in a casual photo application is inconvenient, while the same error in law enforcement can have serious consequences. Privacy questions also arise when people are scanned without clear notice or consent. Responsible deployment therefore requires careful testing, limited data retention, human review, and rules defining when the technology may be used.

## 参考翻译

面部识别系统通过比较图像中提取的特征与已存储的人脸数据来识别或验证一个人。它可以用于手机解锁、照片整理或安全检查，但在公共空间中的应用也引发担忧。系统准确率可能随光照、摄像角度、年龄和人群差异而变化，从而产生不同群体错误率不一致的风险。在普通照片应用中，一次误匹配只是带来不便；但在执法场景中，同样的错误可能造成严重后果。如果系统在没有明确告知或取得同意的情况下扫描人脸，也会产生隐私问题。因此，负责任的部署需要充分测试、限制数据保留时间、保留人工审核，并明确技术的使用边界。
