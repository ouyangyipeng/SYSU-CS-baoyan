# Transformer与注意力

## English

> A transformer is a neural network architecture designed to process sequences without handling every item strictly one after another. Its key component is the attention mechanism, which allows the model to assign different levels of importance to different words or tokens. When processing a sentence, the model can directly connect a word with relevant information that appeared much earlier. This makes transformers easier to train in parallel than traditional recurrent neural networks and helps them learn long-range relationships. Transformers now support most large language models and are also used for images, speech, and biological data. However, attention can require substantial memory and computation because the model may compare many tokens with one another.

## 参考翻译

Transformer 是一种处理序列的神经网络架构，不需要严格按照先后顺序逐项处理输入。它的关键组成部分是注意力机制，使模型能够为不同词语或 token 分配不同的重要程度。处理句子时，模型可以直接把当前词与很早以前出现的相关信息联系起来。与传统循环神经网络相比，这种结构更容易并行训练，也更擅长学习长距离关系。如今，大多数大语言模型都以 Transformer 为基础，它也被用于图像、语音和生物数据。不过，由于模型可能需要比较大量 token，注意力机制会消耗较多内存和算力。
