## English

> The Internet of Things, or IoT, connects physical objects such as sensors, household appliances, vehicles, and industrial machines to communication networks. These devices collect information about their surroundings and may exchange data with cloud or edge systems. A smart factory, for example, can monitor equipment temperature and vibration so that failures are detected before a machine stops working. Technologies such as 5G can support large numbers of devices and provide lower communication delay. However, many IoT devices have limited power and computing capacity, which makes strong security and frequent software updates difficult. Designers must therefore consider energy use, network reliability, privacy, and the consequences of a compromised device.

## 参考翻译

物联网，即 IoT，把传感器、家用电器、车辆和工业设备等实体对象连接到通信网络。这些设备收集周围环境的信息，并与云端或边缘系统交换数据。例如，智能工厂可以监测设备的温度和振动，在机器真正停机前发现故障。5G 等技术能够支持大量设备，并提供较低通信延迟。不过，许多物联网设备的电量和算力有限，难以部署强安全机制或频繁更新软件。因此，设计者需要同时考虑能耗、网络可靠性、隐私，以及设备被攻破后可能造成的后果。
