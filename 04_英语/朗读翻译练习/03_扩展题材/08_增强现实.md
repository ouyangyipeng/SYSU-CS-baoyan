# 增强现实

## English

> Augmented reality, or AR, adds digital information to a user’s view of the physical world instead of replacing the world completely. A phone or headset uses cameras and sensors to understand the surrounding scene and determine where virtual objects should appear. In a shopping application, AR may place furniture in a room or jewelry on a person while updating the image as the camera moves. Accurate tracking and scale estimation are necessary for the virtual object to remain in the correct position. Poor lighting, blocked body parts, and limited processing power can reduce realism. Because AR devices continuously observe the environment, applications must also explain how images and location data are processed and stored.

## 参考翻译

增强现实，即 AR，把数字信息叠加到用户看到的真实世界中，而不是完全替代现实环境。手机或头戴设备利用摄像头和传感器理解周围场景，并判断虚拟物体应该出现在哪里。在购物应用中，AR 可以把家具放入房间，或把珠宝放到用户身上，并在摄像头移动时实时更新画面。准确的追踪和尺度估计能够让虚拟物体保持在正确位置。光照不足、身体部位被遮挡和设备算力有限都会降低真实感。由于 AR 设备会持续观察环境，应用还应说明如何处理和保存图像及位置数据。
