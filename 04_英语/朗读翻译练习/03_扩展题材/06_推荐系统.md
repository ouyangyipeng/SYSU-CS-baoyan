# 推荐系统

类型：扩展题材。根据常见计算机英语题材自编的模拟练习，没有原题命中的含义。

涉及公司政策、产业趋势的内容按原练习的历史背景阅读，不作为当前新闻。

## English

> Recommendation systems help users discover products, films, music, or information that may match their interests. One common method, collaborative filtering, looks for patterns among users with similar behavior, while content-based methods compare the properties of items. Streaming and shopping platforms often combine several approaches with information such as viewing history, clicks, and recent context. A good recommender must balance accuracy with diversity, because repeatedly showing only familiar items can limit discovery. New users and new products create a cold-start problem because the system has little behavior data about them. Recommendations may also influence what people buy or watch, so platforms should consider bias, transparency, and the protection of personal data.

## 参考翻译

推荐系统帮助用户发现可能符合兴趣的商品、电影、音乐或信息。常见方法之一是协同过滤，它寻找行为相似用户之间的模式；基于内容的方法则比较不同物品的属性。流媒体和购物平台通常会结合多种方法，并利用观看历史、点击和近期上下文等信息。好的推荐系统需要兼顾准确性和多样性，因为只反复展示熟悉内容会限制用户发现新事物。新用户和新商品缺少行为数据，会形成冷启动问题。推荐还会影响人们购买或观看什么，因此平台需要考虑偏差、透明度和个人数据保护。
