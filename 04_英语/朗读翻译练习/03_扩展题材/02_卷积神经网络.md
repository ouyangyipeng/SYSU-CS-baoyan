## English

> A convolutional neural network, or CNN, is designed mainly for data arranged in a grid, such as a digital image. Instead of connecting every pixel directly to every neuron, a CNN applies small filters that move across the image and detect local patterns. Early layers may respond to edges or colors, while deeper layers combine these simple features into shapes and objects. Because the same filter is reused in many locations, the network requires fewer parameters than a fully connected model of similar size. CNNs are widely used for image classification, medical imaging, and object detection. Their predictions can still fail when lighting, camera angle, or image quality differs greatly from the training data.

## 参考翻译

卷积神经网络，即 CNN，主要处理数字图像这类网格状数据。它不会把每个像素直接连接到所有神经元，而是使用在图像上移动的小型滤波器来检测局部模式。浅层可能识别边缘或颜色，深层则把这些简单特征组合成形状和物体。由于同一滤波器会在许多位置重复使用，CNN 通常比相近规模的全连接模型需要更少参数。CNN 广泛用于图像分类、医学影像和目标检测。不过，当光照、拍摄角度或图像质量与训练数据差异很大时，模型仍可能预测失败。
