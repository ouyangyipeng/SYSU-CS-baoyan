# 无人机控制

类型：现场题材复原。按 2026 年考生回忆的题材自编，不是考场原文。

涉及公司政策、产业趋势的内容按原练习的历史背景阅读，不作为当前新闻。

## English

> An unmanned aerial vehicle must continually estimate its motion before it can follow a route safely. An inertial measurement unit, or IMU, measures acceleration and rotation at a high rate, while GPS provides a slower estimate of global position. The flight controller combines these sensor readings to estimate the drone’s attitude, velocity, and location. A feedback controller then compares the desired motion with the measured state and adjusts the motors to reduce the error. Wind, sensor noise, communication delay, and limited battery power can all make control more difficult. More advanced systems may use model predictive control to consider future constraints, but they require greater computing power and an accurate model of the vehicle.

## 参考翻译

无人机要安全沿路线飞行，必须持续估计自身运动状态。惯性测量单元，即 IMU，以较高频率测量加速度和旋转，GPS 则以较低频率提供全局位置估计。飞行控制器融合这些传感器读数，估计无人机的姿态、速度和位置。反馈控制器随后比较期望运动和测得状态，并调整电机以减小误差。风、传感器噪声、通信延迟和有限电量都会增加控制难度。更先进的系统可使用模型预测控制来考虑未来约束，但这需要更高算力和更准确的飞行器模型。
