# AI产业与大数字

类型：现场题材复原。按 2026 年考生回忆的题材自编，不是考场原文。

涉及公司政策、产业趋势的内容按原练习的历史背景阅读，不作为当前新闻。

## English

> A technology company said its artificial intelligence services were used by 120 million people last year, up from 75 million a year earlier. Revenue from the business rose by 35 percent to 8.4 billion dollars, while the company invested another 3.5 billion dollars in data centers and AI chips. Executives expect demand to keep growing, but they also warned that training and operating large models require enormous amounts of computing power and electricity. The company plans to improve efficiency so that each request uses less energy, even as the total number of users increases.

## 参考翻译

一家科技公司称，其人工智能服务去年有 1.2 亿用户使用，高于前一年的 7500 万。该业务收入增长 35%，达到 84 亿美元；公司还另外投入 35 亿美元建设数据中心并购买 AI 芯片。管理层预计需求仍会增长，但也警告说，训练和运行大模型需要大量算力和电力。公司计划提高效率，使每次请求消耗更少能源，即使用户总数仍在增加。

## 数字速记

- `million` = 百万：`12 million` = 1200 万。
- `billion` = 十亿：`3.5 billion` = 35 亿。
- `trillion` = 万亿：`1.2 trillion` = 1.2 万亿。
- `rose by 20%`：增长了 20%；`rose to 20%`：增长到 20%。
- `from 15% to 20%`：增加 5 个百分点。
- `$8.4 billion`：84 亿美元。
