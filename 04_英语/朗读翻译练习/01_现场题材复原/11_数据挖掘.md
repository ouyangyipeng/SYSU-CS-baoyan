# 数据挖掘

涉及公司政策、产业趋势的内容按原练习的历史背景阅读，不作为当前新闻。

## English

> Data mining is the process of finding useful patterns, relationships, or unusual events in large data sets. It combines methods from statistics, machine learning, and database systems to turn raw records into information that can support decisions. A retailer may discover which products are often bought together, while a bank may look for transactions that differ from normal behavior. The process usually includes selecting data, cleaning errors, building a model, and evaluating whether the discovered pattern is meaningful. A pattern can appear statistically strong and still be misleading if the data is biased or the question is poorly defined. Human judgment therefore remains important when data-mining results are interpreted and used.

## 参考翻译

数据挖掘是从大型数据集中发现有用模式、关系或异常事件的过程。它结合统计学、机器学习和数据库系统的方法，把原始记录转化为能够支持决策的信息。零售商可以发现哪些商品经常被一起购买，银行则可以寻找与正常行为不同的交易。该过程通常包括选择数据、清理错误、建立模型，以及评价发现的模式是否有意义。即使一个模式在统计上看起来很强，如果数据有偏差或问题定义不当，也可能产生误导。因此，在解释和使用数据挖掘结果时，人类判断仍然重要。
