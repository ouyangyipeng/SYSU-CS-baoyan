# 浏览器与第三方Cookie

类型：现场题材复原。按 2026 年考生回忆的题材自编，不是考场原文。

涉及公司政策、产业趋势的内容按原练习的历史背景阅读，不作为当前新闻。

## English

> Google changed its long-running plan to remove third-party cookies from the Chrome browser. Such cookies allow advertising companies to follow activity across different websites, so privacy advocates have called for stronger limits. Google had developed a group of alternatives known as the Privacy Sandbox, but publishers, advertisers, and regulators disagreed about whether the proposals protected users fairly. The company later said Chrome would continue to offer users a choice about third-party cookies rather than immediately removing them for everyone. Supporters welcomed the attention to privacy, while critics worried that the change created further uncertainty for the advertising industry. The debate shows how difficult it is to reduce online tracking without disrupting services that depend on advertising.

## 参考翻译

Google 调整了长期以来从 Chrome 浏览器中移除第三方 Cookie 的计划。第三方 Cookie 可以让广告公司跨网站追踪用户活动，因此隐私倡导者一直要求加强限制。Google 曾开发一组名为 Privacy Sandbox 的替代方案，但出版商、广告商和监管机构对这些方案能否公平保护用户存在分歧。公司后来表示，Chrome 将继续让用户选择如何处理第三方 Cookie，而不是立即对所有人统一移除。支持者认可其对隐私的关注，批评者则担心政策变化给广告行业带来更多不确定性。这场争论说明，在不影响依赖广告的服务的情况下减少网络追踪并不容易。
