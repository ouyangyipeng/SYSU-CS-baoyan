# 珠宝虚拟试戴

## English

> A virtual try-on application allows shoppers to see how jewelry may look without visiting a store. Using a phone camera, the software first detects a hand, face, ear, or neck and estimates its position in the image. It then places a digital model of a ring, necklace, or pair of earrings on the correct body part and updates the image as the user moves. The experience can make online shopping more convenient and reduce uncertainty about size or style. However, realistic results require accurate tracking, suitable lighting, and correct estimates of scale. The application should also explain how camera images are processed and whether any personal data is stored.

## 参考翻译

虚拟试戴应用让消费者无需到店，也能查看珠宝佩戴后的大致效果。软件使用手机摄像头，先检测手、脸、耳朵或颈部，并估计其在图像中的位置。随后，它把戒指、项链或耳环的数字模型放在相应部位，并随用户移动实时更新。该体验能让网购更加方便，也能减少消费者对尺寸或风格的不确定感。不过，真实效果依赖准确追踪、适当光照和正确的比例估计。应用还应说明摄像头图像如何处理，以及是否保存个人数据。
