# 电子信息技术

## English

> Electronic information systems collect, transmit, process, and store information by representing it as electrical or digital signals. A sensor first converts a physical quantity, such as sound, light, or temperature, into a signal that a circuit can handle. The signal may then be sampled and converted into digital data before it is sent through a wired or wireless communication channel. Noise and interference can alter the signal during transmission, so systems use filtering and error-control methods to recover useful information. At the receiving end, a processor analyzes the data and may produce an image, a control command, or a stored record. Modern communication devices combine all of these stages in a small, energy-efficient system.

## 参考翻译

电子信息系统通过把信息表示为电信号或数字信号，完成信息的采集、传输、处理和存储。传感器先把声音、光或温度等物理量转换成电路能够处理的信号。随后，信号可以被采样并转换为数字数据，再通过有线或无线通信信道发送。噪声和干扰可能在传输过程中改变信号，因此系统会利用滤波和差错控制方法恢复有用信息。接收端的处理器分析数据，并生成图像、控制命令或存储记录。现代通信设备把这些阶段集成在小型、节能的系统中。
