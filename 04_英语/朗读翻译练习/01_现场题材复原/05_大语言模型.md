## English

> Large language models are neural networks trained on enormous collections of text and other data. Most modern models use the transformer architecture, which can learn relationships between words even when they are far apart in a sentence. During generation, the model repeatedly estimates which token is most likely to come next, producing an answer one token at a time. This simple objective allows the model to summarize documents, write code, and answer questions, but it does not guarantee that every statement is true. A model may generate plausible information that is unsupported by its training data or the user’s context. Reliable applications therefore add retrieval, tool use, verification, or human review instead of trusting the model alone.

## 参考翻译

大语言模型是在海量文本和其他数据上训练的神经网络。多数现代模型采用 Transformer 架构，它能够学习句子中相距很远的词语之间的关系。在生成过程中，模型不断估计下一个最可能出现的 token，并逐个 token 形成回答。这个看似简单的目标使模型可以总结文档、编写代码和回答问题，但并不能保证每句话都真实。模型可能生成看起来合理、却缺少训练数据或用户上下文支持的信息。因此，可靠应用通常还会加入检索、工具调用、验证或人工审核。
