# 实时操作系统

类型：现场题材复原。按 2026 年考生回忆的题材自编，不是考场原文。

涉及公司政策、产业趋势的内容按原练习的历史背景阅读，不作为当前新闻。

## English

> A real-time operating system, or RTOS, is built for applications that must respond to events within predictable time limits. It schedules tasks according to priorities and aims to keep the delay between an event and the required response both short and bounded. This property matters in industrial controllers, medical devices, vehicles, and robots, where a late result may be as harmful as an incorrect one. An RTOS is not simply an operating system that runs very fast; predictability is more important than average speed. Designers must consider interrupt latency, task deadlines, and competition for shared resources. They also need methods such as priority inheritance to reduce problems caused when a low-priority task blocks a more urgent one.

## 参考翻译

实时操作系统，即 RTOS，面向必须在可预测时间限制内响应事件的应用。它根据优先级调度任务，并尽量保证从事件发生到系统响应之间的延迟既短又有明确上界。对于工业控制器、医疗设备、车辆和机器人而言，这一点很重要，因为结果过晚可能和结果错误一样危险。RTOS 并不只是“运行得很快”的操作系统；可预测性比平均速度更重要。设计者需要考虑中断延迟、任务截止时间和共享资源竞争，还可通过优先级继承等方法缓解低优先级任务阻塞紧急任务的问题。
