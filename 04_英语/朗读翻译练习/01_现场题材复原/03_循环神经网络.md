# 循环神经网络

## English

> A recurrent neural network, or RNN, is designed for sequential data such as speech, text, and time-series measurements. Unlike a feedforward network that treats each input independently, an RNN passes information from one time step to the next through a hidden state. This internal state gives the network a limited form of memory and helps it use earlier inputs when processing later ones. Standard RNNs, however, may have difficulty learning long-term relationships because gradients can become very small or very large during training. Variants such as LSTM and GRU introduce gates that control what information should be kept or forgotten. Transformers have replaced RNNs in many language tasks, but RNNs remain useful in some compact or streaming systems.

## 参考翻译

循环神经网络，即 RNN，专门处理语音、文本和时间序列测量等序列数据。前馈网络通常独立处理每个输入，而 RNN 会通过隐藏状态把一个时间步的信息传递到下一个时间步。这种内部状态为网络提供了有限的记忆，使其处理后续输入时能够利用之前的信息。不过，普通 RNN 在训练中可能出现梯度过小或过大的问题，因此难以学习长期依赖。LSTM 和 GRU 等变体通过门控机制决定保留或遗忘哪些信息。虽然 Transformer 已在许多语言任务中取代 RNN，但 RNN 在部分紧凑或流式系统中仍有用途。
