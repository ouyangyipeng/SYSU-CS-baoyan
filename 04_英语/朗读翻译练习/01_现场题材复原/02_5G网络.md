## English

> Fifth-generation mobile networks, commonly known as 5G, are designed to provide higher data rates, lower latency, and support for far more connected devices than earlier networks. These improvements are useful not only for faster phone downloads but also for industrial sensors, connected vehicles, and remote control systems. Some 5G services use higher-frequency signals, which can carry more data but may travel shorter distances and be blocked more easily by buildings. Operators therefore need a denser network of base stations in some areas. Actual performance still depends on coverage, available spectrum, and the number of users sharing the network. For this reason, the 5G experience can vary greatly between locations.

## 参考翻译

第五代移动通信网络通常称为 5G，其目标是比前几代网络提供更高的数据速率、更低的延迟，并支持更多联网设备。这些改进不仅能提升手机下载速度，也适用于工业传感器、联网车辆和远程控制系统。部分 5G 服务使用更高频率的信号，虽然能传输更多数据，但传播距离可能更短，也更容易被建筑物遮挡。因此，运营商在某些区域需要建设更密集的基站。实际性能还会受到覆盖范围、可用频谱和共享网络的用户数量影响，所以不同地点的 5G 体验可能差异很大。
