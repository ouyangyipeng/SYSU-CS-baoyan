# 流媒体账号共享政策

类型：现场题材复原。按 2026 年考生回忆的题材自编，不是考场原文。

涉及公司政策、产业趋势的内容按原练习的历史背景阅读，不作为当前新闻。

## English

> Netflix introduced rules limiting the free use of one account by people who live in different households. The company says a standard account is intended for one household, although family members living together can still use separate profiles and devices. A person outside the household may need to create a new account or be added as a paid extra member where that option is available. Netflix argues that widespread password sharing reduces the revenue needed to produce and license content. Critics point out that the idea of a household can be difficult to apply to students, travelers, or families living in more than one place. The policy shows how an online service must balance user convenience with its business model.

## 参考翻译

Netflix 推出规定，限制居住在不同家庭的人免费共用一个账号。公司表示，普通账号原则上供一个家庭使用，但共同居住的家庭成员仍可使用不同个人资料和设备。家庭之外的人可能需要新建账号，或在支持的地区付费添加为额外成员。Netflix 认为，大规模共享密码会减少制作和授权内容所需的收入。批评者指出，对学生、旅行者或分居多地的家庭而言，“一个家庭”的定义很难直接适用。这项政策体现了在线服务需要在用户便利和商业模式之间权衡。
