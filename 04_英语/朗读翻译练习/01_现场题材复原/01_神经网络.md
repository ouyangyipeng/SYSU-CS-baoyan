# 神经网络

## English

> An artificial neural network is a computing model made of connected processing units, often called neurons. Each connection has a weight that determines how strongly one unit influences another, and training adjusts these weights to reduce prediction errors. In a deep network, information passes through several layers, allowing the model to learn increasingly complex patterns. Early layers in an image model may detect edges, while later layers recognize shapes or objects. Neural networks are powerful, but they usually require large data sets and considerable computing resources. They may also make confident mistakes, so accuracy alone does not guarantee that a model is reliable in every situation.

## 参考翻译

人工神经网络是一种由相互连接的处理单元构成的计算模型，这些单元通常称为神经元。每条连接都有一个权重，用于决定一个单元对另一个单元的影响强度；训练过程通过调整这些权重来减少预测误差。在深度网络中，信息会经过多个层级，使模型逐步学习更复杂的模式。图像模型的浅层可能识别边缘，深层则识别形状或物体。神经网络功能强大，但通常需要大量数据和计算资源，而且也可能非常自信地犯错，因此高准确率并不等于在所有场景都可靠。
